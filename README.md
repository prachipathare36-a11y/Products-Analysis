# 📖 Story of the Data

## 📌 About the Dataset

This dataset contains Amazon product sales-related information including product categories, pricing, customer reviews, shipment details, and order timelines.  
The data helps analyze customer buying patterns, product demand, and sales performance across different time periods.  
The project focuses on identifying product trends and business opportunities using data visualization techniques.


## 🔍 Business Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Trend Analysis
Analyzed monthly and quarterly sales trends to identify high-demand periods and seasonal sales growth patterns.

### 2. Product Category Performance
Compared product categories to identify top-performing and low-performing product segments.

### 3. Shipment & Customer Review Analysis
Evaluated shipment performance and customer review counts to understand operational efficiency and customer engagement.


# ✅ Key Conclusions

📌 Top-performing product categories contributed approximately **40%+** of overall sales revenue.

📌 Monthly sales trends showed nearly **25% higher sales growth** during peak shopping periods.

📌 Products with higher customer reviews generated around **30% more sales engagement** compared to low-review products.


# 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories
- Improve shipment efficiency during peak sales periods
- Promote highly reviewed products for better customer conversion
- Use trend analysis for inventory and demand forecasting
